Why ragi bites

Rooted in Odisha.
Made for local shelves.

Ragi, known as mandia in Odisha, is at the heart of this idea. The team proposes sourcing millets and other ingredients nearby, then selling through local shops, direct orders and simple online ordering in Bhubaneswar.

The aim is an approachable local range with clear labels and hygienic packing. Product recipes, shelf life and any nutrition claims would need to be checked before launch.

From ingredients to packet

Small batches, shared equipment

The plan uses one small room and a common set of basic machines for all four products.

01

Source & check

Buy ingredients in small lots, check quality and moisture, and keep a record of suppliers.

02

Mix, bake or roast

Biscuits and cookies are baked; millet mixture is roasted; cereal is cooked, shaped and dried.

03

Cool & pack

Allow baked or cooked products to cool fully, then weigh and seal in food-grade packaging.

04

Label & deliver

Use clear product labels and distribute through nearby shops and direct local orders.
